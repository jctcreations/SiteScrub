Questions families ask

Good to know before story time

Who is Cory Tarwater?

I’m a children’s author from Utah. I write picture books. So far there are three: The Grumble-Gator’s Greatest Raft, The Grumble-Gator’s Bravest Choice, and Under the Same Sky. The stories come from my own growing-up years and from a simple belief that kindness changes people.

What makes this anti-bullying book different?

Most bully stories end with the bully losing. Not this one. Toby doesn’t hit back. He offers a kind word and an invite to build a raft together. Barnaby changes. Kids see that people can grow, and that their kindness can be the reason.

Are the printables really free?

Yes. Every coloring page, bookmark, and activity on the printables page downloads free. No sign-up. Want them all at once? Join the Story Circle and I’ll send you the Story-Time Kit, every printable in one download.

How do I read it with my child?

Slow down and let them talk. The free Family Reading Guide helps. It has read-aloud tips, six pause-and-talk moments, conversation starters by age, and three at-home activities.

Can Cory visit my classroom or library?

Yes. I do author story-time visits for classrooms and libraries. Teachers can also grab the free educator kit and discussion guide on the For Educators page. Or reach me on the contact page.

Where can I buy the books?

All three books are on Amazon, including the Under the Same Sky hardcover. You can also buy them direct from me, printed to order. Outside the US? The where-to-buy page finds a store near you.

Which book should I start with?

For the pond stories, start with The Grumble-Gator’s Greatest Raft, then read Book 2. Under the Same Sky stands on its own. It’s a sweet one to share with grandparents. See them all on the Bookshelf.
