The Grumble-Gator's Greatest Raft

A Tale of Toby, Barnaby, and the Sunny-Stream Pond

★★★★★

He was supposed to be the enemy. He became the friend who learned his strength was for building, not breaking.

At the shimmering center of Sunny-Stream Pond lives Toby, a kind young turtle. At its shadowy edge lives Barnaby, a Grumble-Gator with a splashy tail and a grumpy heart. When Barnaby’s teasing goes too far, Toby remembers his father’s words and chooses a kind word over a hard shell. What happens next turns an enemy into the pond’s greatest raft-builder. It’s a gentle anti-bullying story about kindness, courage, and standing up to a bully. It makes a great read-aloud for ages 3–8, starring a grumpy alligator kids can’t help but root for.

Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions

What age group is it for?

It’s written for children ages 3–8 and shines as a read-aloud book for preschool and early elementary.

What is the book about?

A kind turtle named Toby is bullied by Barnaby, a grumpy alligator. Instead of hitting back, Toby offers a kind word and invites him to build a raft. It’s a gentle lesson in empathy, peaceful courage, and change.

Is this a good book about bullying for young children?

Yes. It’s a gentle anti-bullying picture book that shows kids how to respond to a bully with kindness and courage instead of hitting back. The message is warm and hopeful, with nothing scary, and it’s made for ages 3–8.

Is it good for classrooms and social-emotional learning?

Yes. Teachers and homeschoolers use it as a read-aloud for kindness, empathy, and social-emotional learning (SEL) lessons, and it pairs well with our free kindness printables.

How much is it and where can I buy it?

It’s available in paperback, hardcover, and eBook. Pick your country on our where-to-buy page and we’ll point you to the nearest store, from Amazon to independent bookshops worldwide.

Is it part of a series?

Yes. It’s the first book set in Sunny-Stream Pond, and Book 2, The Grumble-Gator’s Bravest Choice, is out now.
